## ActivityGhosts

Pace Partners uses
[ActivityGhosts by oldnapalm](https://github.com/oldnapalm/ActivityGhosts) as a
design scaffold. Its FIT-to-GTA coordinate conversion and spawned-rider replay
approach informed this project; the replay and control path was substantially
reworked for dense route geometry, FIT elevation, distance-domain progression
